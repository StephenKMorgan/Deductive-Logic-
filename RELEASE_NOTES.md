Therefore: deductive logic proofs, one line at a time.

**What's new in 3.2.0**
- Truth Trees: test arguments by stacking and branching formulas and closing contradictions; open branches show the counterexample
- Possible Worlds (modal logic): □ and ◇ on worlds and arrows you draw, axioms K, T, 4 and B, and "it can't be done" challenges
- Logic of Time: always, eventually, next and until on a timeline, plus safety, liveness and model checking
- Constructive Logic: why P ∨ ¬P isn't free, countermodels where knowledge grows, and proofs as programs
- Logic about Logic: soundness, completeness, undecidability, the halting problem and Gödel's incompleteness theorems
- Proofs About Numbers: induction, broken inductions and strong induction
- Your Proofs in Lean: the rules you know as Lean code, and every finished propositional puzzle now shows "Your proof in Lean" on the results screen, with a button to open it in Lean's web editor
- Everyday Arguments: hidden premises, formal and informal fallacies, and valid vs sound vs strong
- Descriptions and Counting (Predicate tab): "the F", at most one, exactly one, and proofs with several quantifiers
- New lesson tools: put-in-order steps, and fresh generated practice sets for the new courses

**In the app**
- 19 courses on the Learn tab and 7 on the Predicate tab
- Games: Truth-tellers & Liars, Valid or Not?, Switchboard and Solver
- Daily proofs with a calendar, streaks and a stats screen
